# Piano path: intermediate to Grade 7, pop and classical (round 2, 2026-10-27)

Round 2 of `context/research/curriculum-2026-10/piano.md`. Scope: pop, rock, ballads, film and game themes, classical repertoire, technique, accompaniment and reading. Jazz (standards, shells, rootless voicings, comping, improvising over changes) lives in the separate Jazz path; this file links to it where the skills meet. Every MuseScore link below was opened in the Orca browser on 2026-10-27 (search result pages show each score's level tag, instrumentation and rating, which is what the "level" and "rating" notes quote).

## 1. Target and scope

**Done means:** the owner can (a) play a 20-minute recital of five pieces, two of them classical at ABRSM Grade 6 to 7 and three pop or film pieces from full piano-solo arrangements, in performance mode at full tempo; (b) play any pop song from a chord chart or lead sheet with a chosen accompaniment pattern, an intro and an ending, in at least three keys; (c) play all major and minor scales and arpeggios (plus dominant and diminished sevenths) hands together over four octaves at ABRSM Grade 7 speed (scales half note = 80, four notes a click, about 5.3 notes a second; arpeggios half note = 56) with the app's fingering; (d) sight-read a Grade 5 grand-staff piece at 80% or better and a pop chord chart at first sight.

**External benchmark:** ABRSM piano Grade 7 for pieces, scales and sight-reading (two grades below exam level is the norm for sight-reading, as Trinity sets it); Pianote Method level 9 and Piano With Jonny's pop accompaniment patterns for pop; RCM Level 8 as a cross-check for scale speeds.

**Starting point:** intermediate, reads both clefs. Round 1 guessed ABRSM Grade 3 to 4. The owner chose the full curriculum with nothing skipped: the check-in level below turns what is already solid into quick reviews instead.

**How long (honest):** adult learners at 30 minutes a day typically need 8 to 12 months per ABRSM grade above Grade 5. From Grade 3 to Grade 7 that is about 30 to 40 months at 30 minutes a day, or 18 to 24 months at 60 minutes. Pop goals arrive sooner: chord-chart playing with five patterns by level 5 (about 9 to 12 months at 30 minutes), gospel and boogie by level 6. Level by level at 30 minutes a day: L1 to L3 about 2 months each, L4 to L6 about 3 months each, L7 to L10 about 4 months each. The path is built so that 10-minute days still move one thing (one scale key, one pattern, one bar loop).

**Daily shape at 30 minutes (suggested):** 6 minutes technique (one scale family, rotating keys), 4 minutes reading, 4 minutes pattern or style drill, 14 minutes on one shelf song (new passage first, then yesterday's passage in performance mode), 2 minutes ear (from the Ear path). One new motor passage per session, reviewed the next day (Simmons and Duke 2006).

**Step rules used throughout:**
- "Clean run" = 100% correct pitches, timing evenness 85% or better (the Scales drill's evenness score), at or above the stated tempo.
- Every step passes on two separate days (learning-engine R1), not on one good afternoon.
- A shelf song "passes" when Song Studio performance mode scores 90% or better at 100% tempo on two days, with no stops. Wait mode and fix-the-spot loops are the way there; they never count as the pass.
- Grade speeds are ABRSM 2025-26 (already in `technique.ts` as `ABRSM_SPEEDS`). Fingering is the app's (`piano-fingering.ts`), shown on the staff and keys.

**App drills referenced:** Scales (`/music/scales`, pattern scale, arpeggio, broken, blocked; ABRSM grade speeds; hands; span one or two octaves), Two hands (`/music/two-hand`, six patterns), Voicings (`/music/voicings`, variants cadence, closest, inversions, major, minor), Pedal (`/music/pedal`), Reading (`/music/reading`, flashcards and sight-reading Primer to Level 3), Rhythm (`/music/learn/rhythm?mode=groove`), Phrases (`/music/phrases`, piano items incl. Alberti, boogie bass, blues shuffle LH, I vi IV V, Canon chords), Jam (`/music/jam`, pop-axis, blues and other progressions), Ear (`/music/ear/...`), Song Studio (`/music/library` import, `/music/songs/[id]` wait and performance modes, fix-the-spot). NEW marks a drill or option the app lacks; section 4 ranks them.

**About the shelves:** 6 to 12 choices per level, pop, rock, film and game, Einaudi and classical mixed. Pick any 3 (at least one classical and one pop) to pass the level. "Official" means a paid Hal Leonard, Faber or ArrangeMe score (MuseScore Pro or a purchase); "community" means a free user upload (download MusicXML with a free account; check against the recording). Difficulty "within level": 1 easier, 2 middle, 3 harder. Where a community arrangement is labelled "Beginner" on MuseScore but sits at a higher shelf, the reason is the musical demand (voicing, pedal, tempo), not the note count.

### Level 0. Check-in (one to two weeks)

Goal: find what is already solid. Nothing is skipped (the owner asked for the full curriculum): a passed check turns its steps into quick reviews, one cold Check run each on two days, so known skills cost minutes, not weeks, and still get spaced review.

| # | Check | Drill and setup | Pass means | If passed, these become reviews |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.1 | Scales at Grade 3 speed | Scales: major C, G, D, F and harmonic minor A, D, E; hands together, two octaves, up-down, Grade 3 (crotchet 80, two notes a click) | 6 of 7 keys clean within two tries | L1 steps 1.1, 1.2 |
| 0.2 | Scales at Grade 4 speed | Same, Grade 4 (crotchet 100), add Bb, Eb, B minor, C minor | 6 of 8 keys clean | L2 steps 2.1, 2.2 |
| 0.3 | Arpeggios | Scales, pattern arpeggio, C, G, F, Am, Dm, hands together, two octaves, Grade 3 | 4 of 5 clean | L1 step 1.3 |
| 0.4 | Reading | Reading, sight-reading Level 3, treble then bass, SASR climb | 80% at Level 3 in both clefs | L1 step 1.9 |
| 0.5 | Cadences | Voicings, variant cadence, 4 keys | 20 chords at 85% or better | L1 step 1.5 |
| 0.6 | Chord chart | Jam, pop-axis (I-V-vi-IV) in C at 80: RH triads in closest position, LH root (self-check until drill 1 exists) | Two choruses without a stop or wrong chord | L1 steps 1.6, 1.7 |
| 0.7 | Piece test | Song Studio, performance mode, after one week of learning each: Grade 2 Minuet in G minor (Petzold), Grade 3 Clementi Op. 36 No. 1 first movement, Grade 4 Burgmüller Ballade Op. 100 No. 15 (links in the shelves below) | 90% at 100% tempo | The passed grade's piece steps become reviews; the path still runs from L1 |

Rule: every level stays in the path. A step marked review needs one clean Check run, cold, on two different days; a review that fails turns back into a normal step.
