# Guitar: the full path, beginner to "pretty well" (round 2, 2026-10-27)

Round 1 (`../guitar.md`) found the drills strong and the path thin: nine steps, no songs, no fingerpicking, lead stopping at one lick. This file is the full path the owner asked for: ten levels, four strands woven through every level, and a song shelf at each level with a MuseScore score for each song.

## 1. Target and scope

**Done means** the owner can, on acoustic or electric:
- strum and sing (or play along with the record) any pop, rock, folk or soul song from a chord sheet, open or barre, at the record's tempo, in 4/4, 6/8 or shuffle feel;
- play riffs and intros from tab at record tempo (Sweet Child O' Mine intro, Day Tripper, Superstition);
- play three or four complete fingerstyle pieces from memory (for example Dust in the Wind, Landslide, Blackbird, Tears in Heaven, and Classical Gas as a stretch), with Travis, arpeggio, pinch and p-i-m-a technique;
- improvise two choruses over a 12-bar blues or a pop loop in any key with the five minor pentatonic boxes, the major pentatonic, bends to pitch and vibrato, and play a learned solo (Comfortably Numb solo 1, Wish You Were Here);
- read a single-line melody in first and fifth position from standard notation, and work out a simple melody or a I-IV-V song by ear.

**Benchmarks.** JustinGuitar Grade 6 (end of the Intermediate course: all five pentatonic boxes, major scale, CAGED, triads, funk, blues and fingerstyle modules) and RGT (LCM) Electric Grade 5 or Rockschool Grade 5 (scale click tempo 80, improvisation over an unseen backing, a set of pieces). The fingerstyle strand goes a little past both on steel-string pop and folk pieces. There is no classical strand: the owner chose pop, rock, folk, blues and jazz.

**Order.** Levels 1 to 6 follow JustinGuitar's module order (Grade 1 chords A D E then minors then C G; Grade 2 sevenths, power chords, blues, first fingerpicking; Grade 3 rhythm and F; Grade 4 barres, major scale, blues lead) so the owner can watch Justin's lesson for any step. From Level 5 the fingerpicking strand runs ahead of Justin (he keeps it light), and Levels 7 to 10 broaden: CAGED and triads, funk, all five boxes, jazz shells and modes, full fingerstyle pieces.

**How long.** At about 30 minutes a day: Levels 1 to 3 take 6 to 8 weeks each, Levels 4 to 6 about 8 to 10 weeks each, Levels 7 to 10 about 10 to 12 weeks each. Total about **22 to 26 months**. At 60 minutes a day, about 14 to 16 months. JustinGuitar's own guide is roughly a year for the Beginner grades and one to two more for Intermediate, which matches. The fingerstyle strand is the long pole: a full piece like Blackbird is 4 to 8 weeks of 10-minute daily slots on its own.

**A practice day at 30 minutes** (the routine should build this): 5 min chord changes (hardest pair from history), 8 min the current song, 7 min the strand of the day (fingerpicking, lead or riff, rotating), 5 min technique (spider or scale), 5 min ear or reading. At 10 minutes: changes plus one song. At 60 to 90: all four strands every day.

**Strand codes** used in every table: **S** chords and strumming, **F** fingerpicking, **L** lead (scales, licks, solos, riffs from single notes), **R** riff or intro (rhythm guitar riffs, power chords, chord-based intros), **T** reading, theory and ear.

**Drill names** used in the steps (all exist unless marked NEW): `chords?mode=build|pick|strum|changes|fingerpick` (Changes is the 60-second One Minute Changes clock; Fingerpick has 15 guitar patterns and 9 guitar song presets), `strum` (strum a progression, chord following), `rhythm` (read-and-tap, echo), `fretboard?mode=spider|positions|note-finder|interval-recall|legato|bends`, `scales`, `licks`, `phrases`, `jam`, `reading`, `ear`, `circle`, and **Studio** (Song Studio: import the MuseScore MusicXML, wait mode then performance mode, fix-the-spot loops). Studio is the default home for every shelf song: download the score from MuseScore, import, practise in wait mode, then performance mode at 70%, 85%, 100%.

**"Clean"** throughout means: the drill's grade at 90% or better (notes on time and in tune, all strings ringing for chords), and the run counts only if it happens on **two different days** (Simmons 2012: accuracy gains need a sleep gap).

**About the scores.** Every song was searched on musescore.com in the Orca browser on 2026-10-27. "OFF" marks an official (paid, or included in MuseScore Pro+) score, almost always Hal Leonard "Easy Guitar with TAB" or "Guitar Tab" (the full transcription). "Community" scores are free user uploads; I chose ones whose title or instrument field says solo guitar or tab, with the most views. I confirmed each from the search result card (title, arranger, instrument field "Solo Guitar", views); I then opened 28 of the community score pages to check instrument (all are "Guitar (Solo)" except one scored as piano, marked) and length; where a community score is an excerpt (intro, riff, 16 to 25 bars) the table says so. The official scores I confirmed from the result card only. Where the official easy arrangement is the better fit for the level I give it first.
