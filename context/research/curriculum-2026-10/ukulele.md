# Ukulele: curricula, what the app has, and a Ukulele Path (2026-10-27)

Builds on `context/research/guitar-ukulele-drills.md` (LCM and RSL syllabi, strum and campanella notes, mic limits). That research is not repeated here. "Thin" marks a source I could only see through a search summary or that is marketing.

## 1. The best programs and what makes them work

### Song-first beginner courses (the early win)

- **Andy Guitar, Ukulele Level 1 to 5.** Level 1: one-finger chords, strumming, F and G with changes, then four songs (La Bamba, You Are My Sunshine, Smoke on the Water, 9 to 5), a practice routine and a "when to move on" lesson. Level 2 adds D, Dm, Em, the C scale and a capo with five more songs. A level should take "2 days to 2/3 weeks max" ([Level 1](https://www.andyguitar.co.uk/courses/ukulele-level-1), [Level 2](https://www.andyguitar.co.uk/courses/ukulele-level-2), [10 day course](https://www.andyguitar.co.uk/courses/10-day-ukulele-course)). Why it works: a real song in the first sitting, an exit test per level, and riffs beside strummed songs from level 1.
- **Cynthia Lin.** Each beginner lesson is one song that teaches one strum: #1 Three Little Birds, #2 I'm Yours (backbeat strum), #3 Don't Worry Be Happy (swing strum), #4 Stand By Me (island strum), #5 Somewhere Over the Rainbow, #6 Brown Eyed Girl, then #7 "How to practise chord changes" ([Class Central index of her lessons](https://www.classcentral.com/course/youtube-all-of-cynthia-s-ukulele-lessons-and-tutorials-56196), [All About Jazz](https://allaboutjazz.com/cynthia-lin-teaching-jazz-ukulele-on-four-strings-of-aloha-lyle-ritz)). Three or four chords per song, and the strum is the new thing each lesson. Her free chord charts are on [Gumroad](https://cynthialin.gumroad.com/l/TOqEf).
- **Ukulele Tricks (Brett McQueen).** "Strumming Tricks" sells itself as "your first 20 songs in 7 styles", then Fingerpicking Tricks, Blues Tricks (strum, solo and sing a blues) and Jazzy Strummin' ([ukuleletricks.com](https://ukuleletricks.com/strumming-tricks/), thin: marketing copy). The idea worth taking: strum styles are the unit of progress, and blues is the first doorway to soloing.
- **UkuleleGo.** Short technique lessons tied to songs: sixty-second chord changes, five fingerpicking patterns (already in the app), the triplet strum ("keep your thumb held high" on the index down, wrist rotation brings the thumb down, index up; only speed up after a few clean bars) ([triplet strum](https://ukulelego.com/lessons/triplet-strum-ukulele/)), a campanella lesson on London Bridge needing only two fretted notes ([campanella](https://ukulelego.com/lessons/a-campanella-lesson/amp/)) and song lessons such as Mad World and Can't Help Falling in Love ([lessons](https://ukulelego.com/lessons/)).
- **Ukulele Underground and Bernadette Teaches Music.** I found no published syllabus for either ([Rock Class 101](https://rockclass101.com/pathways/) mentions Bernadette's 30-day challenge, thin). Treat them as song libraries, not sequences.

### Reading and ensemble methods

- **James Hill Ukulele Initiative and *Ukulele in the Classroom*** (Hill and Chalmers Doane). Each level covers "singing, strumming, melody picking, note reading, and music theory". Level 1: C and F scales, basic chords, the pentatonic scale and 16 beginner pieces built on open-string melodies. Level 2: chromatic melody and harmony, dominant 7ths, secondary dominants, 20 arrangements. Level 3: minor keys, transposition, flatpicking and tremolo ([uketropolis.com/jhui](https://www.uketropolis.com/jhui)). Hill calls it "the cornerstone of a full music literacy program" ([CBC](https://amp.cbc.ca/lite/story/1.3649865)). Why it fits this owner: the one ukulele method teaching reading, theory and improvisation from day one, with melody picking beside strumming.
- **James Hill, *The Ukulele Way*.** Six books, 61 lessons, melody, harmony and rhythm played together; every lesson filmed in re-entrant and linear tuning ([Uketropolis FAQ](https://www.uketropolis.com/the-ukulele-way-faq)). *Booster Uke* teaches "chord twins" for the beginner plateau ([Elderly](https://www.elderly.com/products/booster-uke)).
- **Hal Leonard Ukulele Method Book 1** (Lil' Rev). Notes on each string, the C major scale, chord families (G, F, Em, Am), standard notation and tab together, strumming, tremolo ([library record](https://link.bedfordnhlibrary.org/resource/hNdqmRPkKS4), [Thomann](https://www.thomann.de/be/hal_leonard_ukulele_method_book_1.htm)). Early pieces are chordless children's tunes ([reviews](https://www.thomann.co.uk/hal_leonard_ukulele_method_book_1_reviews.htm?rating=3)): good for reading, slow for motivation.
- **Graded syllabi** (from the earlier research): LCM chord order C F G Am, then Dm C7 G7 and 3/4, then A D Gm A7 E7; RSL asks for campanella C major pentatonic at Grades 4 to 5 and triple-stroke and split-stroke studies around Grade 5.

### Technique beyond the basics

- **Jake Shimabukuro style.** Relaxed wrist, dynamics, triplet and roll strums, tremolo, chord-melody covers ([MasterClass](https://www.masterclass.com/articles/jake-shimabukuros-tips-for-strumming-a-ukulele), thin). While My Guitar Gently Weeps is in Hal Leonard's *Live in Japan* folio ([Musicroom](https://www.musicroom.com/jake-shimabukuro-jake-shimabukuro-live-in-japan-hl00158305)): an aspiration piece.
- **Campanella.** Consecutive notes on different strings so they ring over each other; needs high-G ([Ukulele Hunt, Jonathan Lewis](https://ukulelehunt.com/2015/04/29/campanella-putting-the-melody-first); [campanella pentatonics](https://andreafortuna.org/2019/03/08/pentatonic-scales-on-ukulele-campanella-patterns/)).
- **Formby triple and split strokes.** Index nail down, thumb down, index up ([Ukulele Magazine](https://ukulelemagazine.com/?p=33347)); the split stroke moves the accent off beat 2.

### Evidence on early wins and song-first learning

- Fender says 90% of new guitar players quit within a year and built Fender Play around songs to fix it (company claim, no published method; [Guitar World](https://guitarworld.com/news/90-percent-of-new-guitarists-abandon-the-instrument-within-a-year-according-to-fender)).
- McPherson (2000), 133 beginners aged 7 to 9: commitment before lessons interacted with practice to predict achievement after nine months; neither alone was enough ([bibbase](https://bibbase.org/network/publication/mcpherson-commitmentandpracticekeyingredientsforachievementduringtheearlystagesoflearningamusicalinstrument-2000), [The Strad](https://www.thestrad.com/study-finds-long-term-goals-are-as-important-to-musical-achievement-as-instrumental-practice/5182.article)). Playing music you care about is the cheapest way to keep commitment up.
- Green's Ear Playing Project: 16 matched pairs aged 10 to 14 learning from recordings without notation; the experimental group "surpassed the control group in every criterion" at post-test; about 80% enjoyed it and wanted ear playing alongside notation ([Baker and Green](https://discovery-pp.ucl.ac.uk/1479500/1/RB19_Playing_by_Ear_in_the_Instrumental_Lesson_Green.pdf), [Musical Futures evaluation, Hallam and Creech](https://discovery-pp.ucl.ac.uk/1507478/2/RB16_Musical_Futures_Hallam_Creech.pdf)). Small samples, children.
- Duke, Simmons and Cash (2009): proportion of correct trials predicts retention (already in the digest). For a beginner this argues for songs at a tempo they can play cleanly, with the tempo ladder doing the rest.

Consensus sequence: C, Am, F, then G or G7 within the first week; down strums, then D DU UDU (island) within the first songs; a backbeat or chuck strum next; 3/4 and triplet feel by the end of the first level; a melody on the C scale alongside chords from the start (JHUI, Hal Leonard, Andy's riffs); fingerpicking with the thumb on string 4 in the second level; dominant 7ths and the Hawaiian vamp and ragtime circle next; campanella, chord melody and blues soloing at intermediate.

## 2. What the app has today

Seen in Orca with the strip on Ukulele, and in code.

- **Paths:** none for ukulele. The Guitar Path's nine table steps plus one added step are all pinned to guitar (`learning-paths.json`, `applies.only: ["guitar"]`), including "Island strum with a mute".
- **Today:** the ukulele routine is String Independence (spider), Ear review, Chord Work, Rhythm (`data/daily-routine.json`). Spider first is the guitar teacher's warm-up, not what any ukulele course starts with; there is no song in the session.
- **Drills listed for Ukulele:** all ear drills, Note reading and Sight-reading (tab hidden by default, good for JHUI-style reading), Rhythm, Scales, Fretboard, Chords (including fingerpicking), Strum a progression, Chord scales, Jazz licks, Jam, Circle, Studio ears, Phrases, Song Studio.
- **Chords:** 12 curated ukulele shapes in LCM order (C F G Am Dm C7 G7 A D Gm A7 E7, `chord-shapes.json`) plus the voicing generator for any chord. Em, Bb, E, Fmaj7, Am7 and the 7th chords of the ragtime circle beyond these (D7, B7) have no curated shape, so they get no change pairs.
- **Strums:** two ukulele patterns in Rhythm read-and-tap: island D DU UDU and a chuck on 2 and 4, both over C Am F G (`rhythm-patterns.json`). Both map to the generic `technique.strumming` skill. No triplet, calypso, swing, 6/8 or split-stroke strum.
- **Fingerpicking:** 13 ukulele patterns (claw, alternating thumb, p i m a, outside in, Travis, fancy eight, rolls, Ohta-San 6/8, pinches) and 4 song presets (Hallelujah, Can't Help Falling in Love, House of the Rising Sun, Scarborough Fair) (`content/technique.ts`). This is better than most courses.
- **Phrases:** 13 ukulele phrases (Twinkle, Mary, Ode to Joy, Jingle Bells, Happy Birthday, Saints, Oh Susanna, Camptown, Amazing Grace, a C to F picking pattern, island strum on C, C Am F G, the Hawaiian vamp D7 G7 C), all public domain or stock vocabulary.
- **Strum a progression:** chord chart from the Jam progressions, followed by chroma, demo strums on beats 1 and 3, no strum pattern, no song presets, and no skill (journal only).
- **Gaps:** no campanella, chord melody, split stroke or triplet strum anywhere in code. Positions mode has only six-string boxes (TASKS says ukulele gets "Switch to guitar"). No ukulele interval-recall skill. Nothing tracks the high-G versus low-G choice beyond the tuning pill.
- **Observation, unconfirmed:** while I had Ukulele picked, opening `/music/strum` showed "switched to Guitar" and the strip changed. Another agent's tab was open on the same dev server, and settings are shared, so this may be a concurrency artefact rather than a bug. Worth one manual check.

Compared with the courses: the app is strong on isolated technique (changes, fingerpicking, reading, ear) and weak on the thing every successful ukulele course leads with: a whole song, strummed in a named pattern, in the first session.

### Proposed Ukulele Path

"From first chords to a song, a melody and a solo." Steps marked new need recommendation 2, 4, 5, 6 or 7.

| Order | Step | Drill and setup | Done means (proposal) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | C, Am, F clean | Chords Build, ukulele, C then Am then F | 3 shapes, 5 clean builds each |
| 2 | Down strums, four chords | Phrases `ukulele.four-chords` at 70, then 90 | 3 runs at 90%, 2 sessions |
| 3 | One-minute changes | Chords Changes, C to Am, Am to F, F to C, C to G7, a change every bar at 60 | 20 clean changes a minute on each pair |
| 4 | Island strum | Rhythm read-and-tap `ukulele-island-strum` at 80 | 5 runs at 75% (the guitar step's bar) |
| 5 | First song (new) | Strum a song: Stand By Me or Riptide preset | 3 full runs at 80%, chord and timing |
| 6 | First melody | Phrases Saints, Ode to Joy (C scale, first position) | 3 runs at 90% |
| 7 | Read it | Sight-reading, ukulele, first position, tab hidden | Reading level climb to level 2 |
| 8 | Chuck and backbeat | Rhythm `ukulele-chuck`, then I'm Yours or Three Little Birds preset (new) | 5 runs at 75% |
| 9 | Second chord set | Chords Changes: Dm, G7, C7, A7, E7, D (LCM Grade 1) | 20 a minute per pair |
| 10 | Thumb and fingers | Fingerpicking claw, then thumb on 4 i m a, then the House of the Rising Sun preset | 3 runs at 80% |
| 11 | Three and triplets (new strums) | 6/8 waltz strum, then Can't Help Falling in Love; triplet strum at 60 | 3 runs at 80% |
| 12 | Hear major and minor | Ear Chord qualities, major/minor only (LCM Step 2) | 20 answers at 80% |
| 13 | The Hawaiian vamp and ragtime circle | Phrases `ukulele.hawaiian-vamp`; Strum a song: Five Foot Two (E7 A7 D7 G7 C) | 3 runs at 80% |
| 14 | Pentatonic on four strings (new positions) | Scales C major pentatonic, then A minor pentatonic, ukulele | 3 runs at 66% at 80 |
| 15 | Solo over the blues | Jam, blues in A, ukulele; then a blues phrase in Check | Phrase 3 runs at 80% (Jam has no mastery) |
| 16 | Campanella (new, high-G) | Campanella C major scale, then London Bridge | 3 runs at 80% |
| 17 | First chord melody (new) | Phrases: Amazing Grace chord melody | 3 runs at 80% |
| 18 | Barre and closed shapes | Chords Changes F to Bb, then Bb to C7 | 15 a minute |
| 19 | Fingerstyle piece | Phrases or Song Studio: Aloha 'Oe or You Are My Sunshine solo | 3 runs at 80% |

Steps 1 to 8 are what Andy Guitar's Level 1 and Cynthia Lin's first four lessons cover; at 15 to 20 minutes a day that is two to three weeks. Ear and Circle of Fifths paths stay linked, not duplicated.

Smaller: Strum and identify should name the deciding string when C and Am are confused (they share two of three pitch classes); add Mad World and You Are My Sunshine to the fingerpicking presets; ask high-G or low-G once on first ukulele use.

## 5. Open questions for the owner

1. Bundling: the earlier phrase policy was public-domain melodies only. Do you want copyrighted song melodies and riffs (Hey Soul Sister intro, Smoke on the Water, Wipeout) written out in the app, or chords and strum only for those?
2. Is your ukulele high-G or low-G, and soprano, concert or tenor? Campanella needs high-G; a low-G player gets linear scales instead.
3. Do you sing while you play? Most ukulele courses assume it; the path could add a "play and sing" self-check step.
4. Which first songs do you actually want? The path's step 5 should be a song you like, picked from the list or named by you.
5. Guitar and ukulele share shapes (ukulele is a guitar's top four strings up a fourth). Should progress on one count toward the other's chord-change steps, or stay separate per instrument (today it is per instrument)?
