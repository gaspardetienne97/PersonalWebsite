# Guitar and ukulele drills research: technique, rhythm, fretboard, syllabi, apps, what a mic can measure (2026-10-13)

Research for a guitar/ukulele practice mode. Input is mostly one microphone (McLeod monophonic pitch, 12-bin chroma, onset detection), sometimes an audio interface, rarely a MIDI guitar (Jamstik, Fishman TriplePlay). Local reference only. Every claim carries a source; "thin" marks a weak source (forum, marketing, search-tool summary of a page I could not open, single small study). Scoring rules in section 7 are my proposals unless a source is named. Several official pages (justinguitar.com lessons, Rockschool AMEB PDFs, Yousician help) returned 403 to direct fetches; where I relied on a search summary of them I say so.

## 0. What one microphone can and cannot measure

| Thing | Mic + McLeod/chroma/onsets | Evidence | MIDI guitar |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pitch of a single note, intonation in cents | Yes | standard pitch tracking | Yes (note + pitch bend) |
| Onset time of a note or strum | Yes, after latency calibration. Rocksmith+ says a well-tuned WASAPI path reaches "about 25 milliseconds" round trip, so latency must be measured, not assumed ([Ubisoft dev diary 2022](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/game/rocksmith/plus/news-updates/1Zcwvdcw0IwhBEYoCGCQtt/rocksmith-dev-diary-february-2022-audio-updates)) | | Jamstik pitch-to-MIDI averages 10 ms, typical 6 to 15 ms (manufacturer, [Jamstik FAQ](https://support.jamstik.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038820812)) |
| Which string/fret a note was played on | No, not reliably. Lab string classifiers on isolated notes reached F = 0.90 (electric guitar, 6 classes) and a full tablature pipeline 82% string accuracy, both offline and instrument-dependent ([Abeßer 2012 via Fraunhofer](https://publica.fraunhofer.de/handle/publica/387270), [Kehling et al. DAFx 2014](https://www.dafx14.fau.de/papers/dafx14_christian_kehling_automatic_tablature_trans.pdf)). A 2026 browser-native preprint attempts it ([Fretiq, arXiv 2607.18303](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.18303)), unreviewed | thin for real-time | Yes: per-string MIDI channels (Jamstik MPE sends strings 1 to 6 on channels 2 to 7, [Jamstik FAQ](https://support.jamstik.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038820812); TriplePlay multi-timbral mode, [KVR forum](https://www.kvraudio.com/forum/viewtopic.php?p=6263737), thin) |
| Chord identity (major/minor/7th) | Roughly, from chroma templates. On real mic recordings a trained CRNN got 90.1% chord accuracy vs 80.4% for Deep Chroma ([arXiv 2508.07973, Table 6](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.07973)). Plain template matching (24 major/minor templates, cosine similarity) is the textbook baseline ([Weiß, Zalkow & Müller, AudioLabs](https://www.audiolabs-erlangen.de/content/resources/MIR/00_2018_CourseMIR_HfM-Karlsruhe/2018_WeissZalkowMueller_MIR_HarmonyAnalysis.pdf)). Rocksmith+ weights the third and sevenths ("that single third that really defines the quality of the chord", Ubisoft dev diary above) | | Yes, exact notes |
| Voicing, inversion, which shape (CAGED, barre vs open) | No from chroma (octave folded). Partly from arpeggiated notes, where the monophonic pitch gives exact octave | | Yes |
| A muted or buzzing string inside a strummed chord | Partly. Chroma misses a dead string only if its pitch class is not doubled elsewhere. Reliable route: arpeggiate the chord and check each expected pitch (Simply Guitar's own advice: "play an arpeggio (one note at a time) and listen if any notes are buzzing or muted", [Simply help](https://guitar-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/5135082-improve-note-recognition-in-simply-guitar)). LCM marks chords "without fretbuzz" ([LCM syllabus](https://lcme.uwl.ac.uk/media/ewrbmogk/electric_and_bass_guitar_syllabus_-_updated_september_2016.pdf)) | | Yes (missing string = missing note) |
| Strum direction (down vs up) | Hard. Best mic result: F1 85.5% down, 79.0% up with a trained CRNN and 50 ms tolerance ([arXiv 2508.07973, Table 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.07973)). Audio-only is hard "due to the very short time difference between the string hits"; a hand-worn IMU got F1 92% down, 85% up ([Murgul & Heizmann, KIT](https://publikationen.bibliothek.kit.edu/1000183784)). Spectrogram methods exist ([Barbancho et al. ISMIR 2014](https://archives.ismir.net/ismir2014/paper/000130.pdf)). With onset + chroma only: do not grade it | | Yes in principle (string-onset order low-to-high vs high-to-low), untested |
| Ghost strokes (hand passes, misses strings) | No, silent by definition. Grade only the timing of the audible strums they keep in place | | No |
| Muted/percussive strums, chucks | Roughly: an onset with weak pitch salience and fast decay | my inference | Partial |
| Palm muting | Partly. A 2026 preprint reports palm-mute F1 97.9% (input type unclear) ([TART, arXiv 2609.11904](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.11904)); a real-time embedded acoustic-guitar classifier got 99.2% pitched-vs-percussive but 56.5% on the full multi-class task ([Stefani & Turchet, DAFx 2022](https://iris.unitn.it/handle/11572/364735)). With our features: shorter decay and less high-frequency energy than the same user's open notes, scored relative to a calibration strum | thin | Partial (velocity, duration) |
| Bends (target pitch, hold, release) | Yes: pitch trajectory in cents vs target. Rocksmith awards bend bonuses ([AVForums quote of official scoring post](https://www.avforums.com/threads/rocksmith-scoring.1769460/), thin) | | Yes (per-string pitch bend; TriplePlay "Smooth" or "Stepped" bend modes, [Guitar Interactive](https://www.guitarinteractivemagazine.com/review/fishman-triple-play-wireless-guitar-controller/)) |
| Vibrato rate and depth | Yes from pitch track. No guitar norms found; string players: rates 4 to 10 Hz, means 5 to 7 Hz, depth about 20 to 35 cents ([summary of string-vibrato literature via arXiv 2512.18162](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.18162v1)), not guitar-specific | thin for guitar | Yes |
| Hammer-on/pull-off/slide vs picked | Partly: pitch change without a strong onset transient = legato; continuous glide = slide. Detection research used a hexaphonic pickup ([Reboursière et al. NIME 2012](https://nime.org/proc/reboursiere2012)) | | Partly |
| Dynamics, accents | Relative loudness per onset (RMS), never absolute | | Velocity, also relative |

## 1. Evidence anchors on practice

- **No controlled study of guitar chord-change speed found.** Searches turned up teaching blogs and one unsourced "43% faster" claim attributed to a Vienna study that I could not trace; treat such numbers as fiction ([Furtados blog](https://furtadosschoolofmusic.com/blog/learning-chords-with-cognitive-science-strategies-for-rapid-chord-progression-mastery/), thin).
- **Auditory feedback matters most for beginners.** 115 students (56 guitarists, 59 pianists), beginner to advanced: first-sight reading was worst; silent (muted) practice helped rhythm and smoothness; listening helped expressive timing and articulation; playing with sound gave fewer intonation errors; "auditory feedback was more relevant for beginners than for advanced students" ([Sci Rep 2022, PMC9671877](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9671877/)).
- **Guitar chord knowledge is visual and orientation-sensitive.** Chord representations depend on the diagram format and orientation and are linked to their sounds and actions ([Crump, Logan & Kimbrough 2012, Music Perception](https://www.crumplab.com/publications/Crump/files/4686/Crump%20et%20al.%20-%202012%20-%20Keeping%20an%20Eye%20on%20Guitar%20Skill%20Visual%20Representat.pdf)). Implication: show chord diagrams in the player's own orientation (left-handed flip).
- **Interleaving.** No guitar-specific blocked-vs-random study found. Music evidence comes from clarinet: random practice was faster at a 24-hour retest in beginners; advanced clarinettists did better with interleaved excerpts; small samples ([Georgia Southern, Stambaugh](https://digitalcommons.georgiasouthern.edu/music-facpubs/2), [Carter & Grahn 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4989027)). See also the piano doc ([piano-drills.md](piano-drills.md) section 0) for Duke 2009 (proportion of correct trials predicts learning; slow down before the error spot) and sleep consolidation.
- **Single-subject guitar timing study.** One player, 11-note melody, 55 trials: timing CV fell from 0.34 to 0.13 ([NHSJS 2026](https://nhsjs.com/2026/how-do-the-dynamics-of-timing-improvement-with-guitar-practice-relate-to-motor-learning-and-trial-and-error-variability/), thin, n=1, student journal).
- **Expert guitarists shape microtiming deliberately.** 21 expert electric guitarists playing "laid-back", "on-the-beat" and "pushed" against a metronome changed stroke velocity and movement duration ([Câmara et al., UiO](https://duo.uio.no/bitstream/handle/10852/79965/MP3801_01_Camara.pdf?sequence=1)). Implication: at advanced levels, consistent offset from the click is a style, not an error; score variability, then mean offset.
- **Timing tolerance.** Strum-transcription research evaluates with a 50 ms window ([arXiv 2508.07973](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.07973)). Tappers lean early (negative mean asynchrony; [Repp & Su 2013 review](https://www.altmetric.com/details/1262980)). Rocksmith deliberately "eased off on the precision so that you can be a little off without being punished" ([Rocksmith dev AMA](https://theriffrepeater.com/rocksmith-developers-are-asked-anything-by-reddit)). No app publishes its window in ms.

## 2. Guitar technique drills: pedagogy and measurability

## 3. Ukulele specifics

- **Chord order.** LCM ukulele chord ranges by grade ([LCM Ukulele Syllabus 2023](https://lcme.uwl.ac.uk/media/qzyhfoue/ukulele-syllabus-2023.pdf)): Step 1 C, F, G, Am (4/4); Step 2 adds Dm, C7, G7 and 3/4; Grade 1 C, F, G, A, D, Am, Dm, Gm, C7, G7, A7, E7; Grade 2 adds Bb, Em, F#m, D7, Cmaj7, Gm7; Grade 3 adds Bm, Fm, B7, Dmaj7, Gmaj7, Em7, Edim7 and 6/8; Grades 4 to 5 full/partial barre and higher-position chords. Teaching sites start with C, then Am and F (keep Am shape, add index), then G7, for C-Am-F-G7 ([Ukulele Magazine](https://ukulelemagazine.com/uke-basics/ukulele-first-4-basic-chords), [Ukulele Tricks](https://ukuleletricks.com/beginner-ukulele-chords/)); James Hill recommends F and C7 first ([LiveUkulele](https://liveukulele.com/lessons/beginner/)). Sixty-second changes also used on uke ([UkuleleGo](https://ukulelego.com/tips/60-second-chord-changes/), thin).
- **LCM uke exam shape** (same syllabus): rhythm study (chord chart with a set 1-bar strum pattern, played twice, from Grade 1 with rhythmic variation the second time), melody or solo piece, unprepared accompaniment to an 8-bar melody, musicianship (clap back a 1-bar rhythm, identify all-major vs all-minor progressions at Step 2, chord types later, then 2-bar and 4-bar rhythms). Grades 6 to 8: "Campanella, harp harmonics, chromatic harmony, slurs, slides". Grade 8 adds a blues chart with chords, composed lead and improvisation.
- **RSL ukulele 2020** (search-tool summary of [AMEB-hosted guide](https://rockschool.ameb.edu.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Instrument-Guides-Ukulele-D-8-2020-3.pdf); page blocked to direct fetch, thin): Debut-level scales group at 65 bpm (C major, C major pentatonic, A minor pentatonic, G major); G4 to G5 scales and arpeggios at 90 bpm including a C major pentatonic "campanella style", barred chords and a strumming exercise to a 90 bpm click; G5 choice of dominant-7 partial-chord study with 16th strumming or a triple-stroke study at 90 bpm; G6 to G8 scales at 100 bpm including a D harmonic minor in campanella style. Chords not directed to click must be "produced clearly with little hesitation".
- **Strums.** Island strum D DU UDU, syncopated on 3 ([UkuTabs](https://ukutabs.com/es/ukulele-guides/how-to-strum-ukulele/), [Rock Class 101](https://rockclass101.com/the-island-strum-reimagined/)); calypso strum down-down-up-up-down-up, second down with the thumb in one ArtistWorks version ([ArtistWorks](https://blog.artistworks.com/how-to-play-ukulele-calypso-strum-lesson/)); island style differs from Trinidadian calypso ([Ukulele Magazine](https://ukulelemagazine.com/?p=37216)).
- **Chuck/chunk.** Heel of the strumming hand mutes the strings right after a strum, percussive "chunk" for reggae/ska ([Got A Ukulele](https://www.gotaukulele.com/2011/02/ukulele-beginners-tips-advanced.html?m=0)). Mic: percussive onset, weak pitch.
- **Split stroke (Formby).** Down strum, then pluck the A and G strings in time, moving the heavy accent off beat 2; index rather than thumb; forearm rotation ([Ukulele Magazine](https://ukulelemagazine.com/?p=28990)). Mic: onset grid + accent position (louder onset on "&").
- **Re-entrant tuning and campanella.** High-G puts all four strings within about an octave (G4 C4 E4 A4); campanella plays consecutive melody notes on different strings so they ring over each other; needs high-G ([Ukulele Hunt](https://ukulelehunt.com/2015/04/29/campanella-putting-the-melody-first), [Andrea Fortuna](https://andreafortuna.org/2019/01/25/diatonic-major-scales-on-ukulele-a-campanella-style-pattern/), [cursa.app](https://cursa.app/en/article/high-g-or-low-g-why-the-ukuleles-fourth-string-breaks-the-rules)). App consequences: (a) the app must know high-G vs low-G, since the same tab gives notes an octave apart on string 4; (b) "let ring" is partly measurable as the previous pitch class staying present in chroma after the next onset; (c) fingerpicking patterns ordered 4-3-2-1 by string are not ordered low-to-high by pitch, so never infer string order from pitch order.
- **Chord melody and methods.** The Ukulele Way (6 books, 61 lessons, every lesson filmed in re-entrant and linear tuning) centres on playing melody, harmony and rhythm together ([Uketropolis FAQ](https://www.uketropolis.com/the-ukulele-way-faq)). Mic: chord melody is polyphonic; grade the top-voice pitch (loudest/highest partial) and onset timing only, flagged experimental.
- **Uke scales.** RSL lists C major, pentatonics, linear and campanella patterns, modes at G6+ (above). Fender Play's ukulele path has fewer levels than guitar (5 vs 7) ([Fender LAUSD help](https://lausd-instructor.fender.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043858892)).
- **No Trinity ukulele syllabus found**; Trinity R&P covers guitar, bass, keys, vocals, drums (search, thin). No ABRSM ukulele syllabus found either; AMEB has one ([AMEB spotlight](https://www.utas.edu.au/australian-music-examinations-board/ameb-news-and-events/ameb/spotlights/spotlight-on-ukulele-syllabus), not opened).

## 4. What apps do

| App | Input | Grading | Structure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Yousician** | Mic | Scores note accuracy and timing; 1 silver to 3 gold stars, gold needs everything correct with good timing ([help article](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/208014795-How-song-score-is-calculated), search summary); no published window or formula. Recognition tips: tune with in-app tuner, cut noise, effects off ([help](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/46277332028817-Guitar-sound-recognition-issues)) | Missions = songs + video + trainers, themed (melody/lead, chords/rhythm, knowledge incl. fretboard), 10 levels; 2021 merged branches into one theme-based path, lessons as video, then exercise, then a short song ([help](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/206930639-How-the-guitar-syllabus-works), [blog 2021](https://yousician.com/blog/new-guitar-syllabus-2021)). Klapuri (MIR researcher) is a Yousician inventor on a song-ID-from-live-audio patent ([US 12,347,408](https://patentsgazette.uspto.gov/week26/OG/html/1536-1/US12347408-20250701.html)) |
| **Rocksmith / Rocksmith+** | Real Tone Cable or mic/interface | 100k base per song split equally per note/chord/strum; "groove" bonus for 3 consecutive notes timed correctly relative to each other (early, on, or late variants); slide, bend, sustain, tremolo, harmonic bonuses; hold-time multiplier ([AVForums quote of official post](https://www.avforums.com/threads/rocksmith-scoring.1769460/), 2013, thin). Dynamic difficulty adds notes or turns single notes into chords when you hit most notes, removes them when you struggle; "when we tried dynamic difficulty off most new players couldn't play at all" ([dev AMA](https://theriffrepeater.com/rocksmith-developers-are-asked-anything-by-reddit)). | **JustinGuitar** | App listens for OMC (counts can be low per users) | OMC count, Note Trainer timed tests | 3 Grades, 23 modules, 204 lessons; Grade 1 = eight open chords A D E C G Am Dm Em plus strumming; modules: A and D, then E, then easy minors + up strums, then Dm + metronome + "THE Pattern", then C ([justinguitar.com/begin](https://justinguitar.com/begin), search summary). | **Guitar Tricks** | None (video) | No grading | Linear Core Learning System: Fundamentals 1 (tuning, chords, strumming, simple melodies), Fundamentals 2 (scales, reading, minor and barre chords), then style tracks ([American Songwriter review](https://americansongwriter.com/guitar-tricks-review), thin) |

Takeaways: every mic-based app tunes detection to be forgiving and none publishes thresholds; Rocksmith's "groove" scores relative timing between consecutive notes, which survives an uncalibrated latency offset; song-first apps (Rocksmith, Simply, Fender Play) scaffold by simplifying the arrangement, exercise-first ones (Yousician missions, JG) by isolating the skill and then applying it in a song.

## 5. Rhythm and timing

- Measurable via onsets: strum/note onset vs click (asynchrony), IOI evenness (SD or CV of inter-onset intervals, same method as piano scales in [piano-drills.md](piano-drills.md)), missing and extra strums against an expected pattern, tempo drift over a run (slope of a line fit to onsets), relative accents (onset RMS on expected strong positions vs others), dynamics changes (RMS trend over bars).
- Not measurable: ghost strokes, strum direction (except via a trained model, section 0), which hand muted.
- Partly: muted strums/chucks (percussive onset, low pitch salience), sustain length (energy decay), "laid-back" vs "pushed" feel (mean asynchrony, valid only after latency calibration).
- Syllabus framing: LCM rhythm playing rewards "fluent chord changes", "even tempo", following dynamics from Grade 1; LCM scales/arpeggios Distinction = "accurate ... clarity and even timing", Merit = "mostly accurate, clear and even", Pass = "some inaccuracies, continuity affected by errors and restarts" ([LCM syllabus](https://lcme.uwl.ac.uk/media/ewrbmogk/electric_and_bass_guitar_syllabus_-_updated_september_2016.pdf), bands 85/75/65%). RSL: exercises start after a 4-click count-in, playing to the click optional to G5 ([RSL 2018 guide](https://www.rockschoolbc.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Instrument-Guides-Electric-Guitar-D-8-2018-1.pdf)).

## 6. Ear and fretboard knowledge, and grade sequencing

**Ear.** RSL ear tests: Debut to G3 melodic recall and chord recognition; G4 to G8 melodic recall and harmonic recall (stated for RSL piano, same format across instruments, [RSL piano G1 copy](https://pdfcoffee.com/piano-g1-edition13may2016-pdf-4-pdf-free.html), thin). LCM uke: higher/lower at Step 1, clap back rhythms, major vs minor progression, chord types and time signature by Grade 3+ (LCM uke syllabus). Guitar-specific ear drills a mic can grade: play back a melody (pitch + rhythm), find the root of a heard chord on the guitar, play the heard interval from a given note. For sequencing and windows reuse [ear-drills-sequencing.md](ear-drills-sequencing.md).

**Fretboard.** Teacher consensus (forum-level, thin): one string at a time starting with 6 or 5, naturals first, octave shapes to transfer, no open strings, accuracy before speed, 5 to 10 minutes daily, weeks to learn ([JG forum](https://community.justinguitar.com/t/how-did-you-learn-your-fretboard-note-names/36003)). CAGED then links chord shapes to scale positions (section 2).

**Grade sequencing with published tempos.** Note values per beat are not stated in these extracts; treat bpm as the exam click, not notes per second.

## 7. Ranked recommendations

Ranking = teacher/syllabus consensus first (no RCT exists for any of these), then how well a mic can grade it. All windows and thresholds are proposals; start lenient (Rocksmith's lesson, section 1) and tighten with level. "Window" = onset error after latency calibration. Gate tempo increases on accuracy, like Rocksmith's accelerator and Guitar Pro's progressive speed, with Duke's "proportion correct" principle (piano doc).

### Guitar (top 12)

| # | Drill | Why | Mic measures | Scoring rule (proposal) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One Minute Changes** on a chord pair, then the 5 hardest pairs | JG core beginner drill; LCM/RSL reward fluent changes | Strums (onsets) labelled by chroma template | Count a change only when consecutive strums classify as the two target chords with confidence above a per-user calibrated cosine threshold. Score = changes/min. Show baseline (6 to 9 typical first try), bands at 30 and 60 (JG). Store per pair; schedule the slowest pairs first |
| 2 | **Chord check by arpeggio** (JG chord-perfect / PFC; Simply's own advice) | Catches muted and buzzing strings chroma cannot see; LCM grades "without fretbuzz" | Monophonic pitch per string, low to high | Each expected pitch present within ±30 cents and sustaining at least ~300 ms (proposal). Score = clean strings / strings. Required before a chord enters OMC; also the barre-chord drill |
| 3 | **Strum pattern to a click** (all downs, then Old Faithful D DU UDU, then 16ths) | Universal; JG learner target 80 bpm | Onsets vs eighth grid, chord per bar | Hit = strum within ±50 ms of an expected slot (tighten to ±30). Penalise extra strums in silent slots. Score = hits/(expected + extra). Tempo ladder +5 bpm after two runs ≥ 90% |
| 4 | **Progression play-along** (rhythm-study style: unseen chart, change on the barline) | LCM rhythm playing; every app's song mode | Chord label per beat, change latency | Per bar: chord correct on beat 1 and change latency (first correct-chroma frame minus barline) ≤ one eighth note. Score = % bars correct; report median change latency |
| 5 | **Pentatonic/major scale positions with a tempo ladder** | RSL/Trinity core technical work at every grade | Pitch sequence, IOI evenness, tempo | Wrong/missing notes via sequence alignment; evenness = CV of IOIs. Pass a rung at ≥ 95% notes and IOI CV under the user's baseline; climb 5 bpm toward the RSL grade tempo |
| 6 | **Alternate picking evenness** (3nps groups, string crossing, spider as warm-up) | Syllabi list alternate picking at G8; spider is common but criticised | Pitch sequence, IOI SD; not pick direction | As 5, emphasise IOI SD per string crossing position (show where it slips, like van Vugt's scale-position profile in piano doc) |
| 7 | **Bend to pitch** (semitone, whole tone, release) | RSL G6 bends study; Rocksmith bend bonus | Pitch trajectory in cents | In tune = peak within ±15 cents of target, close = ±30 (proposal); also time to reach target and drift during hold. Show pitch curve vs target line |
| 8 | **Fretboard note finding** (name shown, play it; then note shown, name it) | Teacher consensus; JG Note Trainer; LCM uke Grade 1 | Pitch class (and octave when constrained) | Correct pitch class within 3 s (tighten to 1 s). To force a string, request a specific octave in a fret range where it occurs once. Spaced repetition per note |
| 9 | **Legato: hammer-on/pull-off trills and runs** | Trinity R&P core; RSL legato studies | Notes, IOI evenness; weak onset at pitch change | Notes and evenness as 5; "legato" tag when onset strength at the new note is below X% of the picked note (calibrated). Do not fail on the tag at first |
| 10 | **Fingerpicking: Travis thumb, then PIMA patterns** | Fender/JG fingerstyle; Trinity/Giuliani arpeggio work | Onset grid, lowest pitch per beat (thumb), pitch per onset | Onset timing as 3; bass alternation correct when the low-register pitch alternates root/other on beats; finger notes checked by pitch class. Fingering not graded |
| 11 | **Vibrato control** | Expression; Rocksmith+ judges it leniently | Rate (Hz), depth (cents), regularity | Report, do not pass/fail: rate and depth with their SD across cycles; target "steady" = cycle-to-cycle rate SD under 15% (proposal) |
| 12 | **Palm-muted power-chord eighths** | RSL G1/G2 power chords, G8 muted strings | Onsets, root+fifth chroma, mute proxy | Timing as 3, chord as root+fifth; mute = decay time below 50% of the user's unmuted calibration strum (proposal, experimental) |

Next tier: triads on string sets and CAGED shapes played as arpeggios (exact pitches verify voicing); melodic recall/interval by ear (reuse ear-drill engine); slides; accents/dynamics in strumming (relative RMS).

### Ukulele (top 6)

| # | Drill | Mic measures | Scoring rule (proposal) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One Minute Changes** over C-Am-F-G7 pairs (LCM Step 1-2 chords) | Chroma per strum | As guitar #1; four-string chords are compact so templates are clean, but C and Am share 2 of 3 pitch classes, so require the distinguishing pitch class (G vs A) to clear a margin |

| 3 | **Chuck strum** (backbeat chucks on 2 and 4) | Percussive onset with low pitch salience and fast decay | Chuck slot hit = onset within window AND chroma confidence below the user's calibrated chord level; strums elsewhere scored as #2 |
| 4 | **Melody playing** (LCM melodies, single notes) | Pitch + onset | Pitch within ±30 cents, onset within ±60 ms; octave check honours the user's high-G/low-G setting |
| 5 | **Campanella C major scale** (high-G only; RSL G4-5, LCM G6+) | Pitch sequence, evenness; ring-over = previous pitch class still present in chroma after next onset | Notes and evenness as guitar #5; ring score = share of notes whose predecessor is still audible at the next onset (experimental) |
| 6 | **Fingerpicking pattern** (thumb on 4th string, i-m-a on 3-2-1, then split stroke at higher level) | Onset grid, pitch per onset | Timing as #2; pitch sequence must match the tuning-specific order (re-entrant: string 4 is not the lowest pitch) |

### MIDI guitar upgrades

With Jamstik/TriplePlay in multi-channel mode the same drills gain: string and fret verification (fretboard drill #8, CAGED positions), exact chord voicing and dead strings (replaces the arpeggio check), bend curves from per-string pitch bend, and possibly strum direction from string onset order (untested). Expect 6 to 15 ms extra latency (Jamstik) and possible wireless penalties of 30 to 40 ms (SOS review via search, thin).

## 8. Gaps

- No published app timing windows (Yousician, Rocksmith, Fender Play), no guitar-specific chord-change or practice-schedule study, no human bend-accuracy norms, no guitar vibrato norms, no tempos for LCM/RGT (handbooks only) or current RSL editions, and RSL note values per click unconfirmed.
- Strum direction, palm muting and string identity need trained models on mic audio; none are worth building before the core timing and chroma drills.
