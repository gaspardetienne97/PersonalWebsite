Recorder: its own destination or inside Create?

  • Recommend: own destination "Record" in the utilities group, reachable in one tap while an instrument is in hand, and openable from any drill's summary ("Record this"). Create keeps sequencing (Beat grid, Piano roll) where a mouse is the instrument.
