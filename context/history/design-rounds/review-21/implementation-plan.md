## D. Physical checks (owner, separate from software gates)

1. MPC recipe (findings.md), ten minutes. Records: the sixteen notes learned, whether Rudiments advance on A01/A03, groove copy-back matched/omitted/extra counts.
2. Piano over MIDI: Two-Hand with both hands, confirm both keys light while held and release together; Reading play-back shows no lingering red once P0-05 lands.
3. Preview: the same long song with Sampled Piano and with Warm Synth; note whether either cuts out and at which bar.
4. Microphone: record ten seconds speaking at 30 cm with the meter (after P2-02); note the peak reading and whether playback is audible at full volume.
5. Tuner recordings (for P1-12): open E2, A2, D3 muted and unmuted; A2 with the neighbouring D3 still ringing and the reverse; E2 with the laptop PSU on and off; the laptop mic at 30 cm and 1 m; ten seconds of room silence; for each, the trusted tuner's reading.
6. Aerophone: deferred by decision; nothing here depends on it.

## E. What to reuse, remove, and not build

- Reuse: `PracticeDrillShell` phases for Demo, `technique-ladder` for every tempo rule, `score-follower` performance mode as the timing engine for licks and comping, `rhythm-pattern` for strums, `DeviceProfile` for every per-device setting (pad map now, offset next), the `assistance`/`source` fields for hints and checks.
- Remove or simplify: the second recorder in Practice Lab (after P2-04); the three drum-voice vocabularies (after P3-03); the `Card.Footer` transport pattern on Song Studio (done); `focus-visible:z-20` on white keys (done); the XP ring as hero (decision).
- Do not build: a global timing snap for unsupported notation (rejected in review 18, still right); a gate or smoothing setting that hides wrong tuner estimates; per-route pad grids or keyboards; a server-side anything for Music; new fonts or a second palette; social features.

## F. The first slice

Ship together, in this order, as one branch on top of this working tree: **P0-01** (hardware validation, port names, E2E) → **P0-02** tests → **P0-05** (wrong-note lifetime) → **P1-04** (sight-reading hint) → **P1-06** (Demo mode, together metric). That is the daily loop for the owner's three instruments: pads that grade the right pad, keys that light and clear honestly, a demonstration before practice, hints that are recorded, and a check that means something. Everything in it reuses existing abstractions; none of it changes stored history, scoring rules or the backup format. Estimated two to three days after the gates pass.

Decisions the owner must make: (1) XP semantics (R31), because Progress view C depends on it; (2) Record as a destination or a tab (P2-04); (3) whether the dark primary with dark ink is acceptable (design.md §7.2), otherwise pick a lighter white-ink primary and accept 4.5:1 only for the largest text; (4) which pad-map preset their MPC program uses, or run the learn flow.
