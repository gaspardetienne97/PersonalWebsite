# Third-Party Notices

## Salamander Grand Piano

The Music app bundles an MP3 anchor subset of **Salamander Grand Piano V3** by
Alexander Holm. The samples are licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

- Original instrument: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
- MP3 conversion source: https://github.com/darosh/samples-piano-mp3

Only eight C-note anchors from velocity layer 8 are included; Tone.js pitch
mapping supplies the notes between them.
